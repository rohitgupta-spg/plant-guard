### 1.0 PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document defines the operational parameters, preventive maintenance schedules, fault diagnostics, consumable parts, and safety protocols for all six-axis articulated welding robots (Asset Class: ROBOT-WELD) installed in the Vindhya Precision Works production facility. Compliance with this procedure is mandatory for all assigned maintenance technicians and welding operators.

---

### 2.0 NORMAL OPERATING RANGES AND ALARM SETPOINTS
Operation outside the parameters defined in Table 2.1 shall result in automated interlock tripping or operator intervention as specified.

**Table 2.1: Operational Limits for ROBOT-WELD**

| Parameter | Normal Operating Range | Warning Alarm Setpoint | Critical Trip / Interlock Setpoint |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Axis 1–3 Gearbox Temp** | $+15^\circ\text{C}$ to $+52^\circ\text{C}$ | $+60^\circ\text{C}$ | $+70^\circ\text{C}$ |
| **Axis 4–6 Wrist Temp** | $+15^\circ\text{C}$ to $+45^\circ\text{C}$ | $+52^\circ\text{C}$ | $+62^\circ\text{C}$ |
| **Welding Power Source Temp**| $+20^\circ\text{C}$ to $+55^\circ\text{C}$ | $+65^\circ\text{C}$ | $+75^\circ\text{C}$ |
| **Arm Vibration (RMS)** | $0.2\text{ mm/s}$ to $1.8\text{ mm/s}$ | $2.5\text{ mm/s}$ | $3.5\text{ mm/s}$ |
| **Shielding Gas Pressure** | $0.22\text{ MPa}$ to $0.28\text{ MPa}$ | $< 0.20\text{ MPa}$ or $> 0.30\text{ MPa}$ | $< 0.15\text{ MPa}$ or $> 0.35\text{ MPa}$ |
| **Pneumatic Brake Supply** | $0.55\text{ MPa}$ to $0.65\text{ MPa}$ | $< 0.50\text{ MPa}$ | $< 0.42\text{ MPa}$ |
| **Welding Current (Arc On)**| $80\text{ A}$ to $320\text{ A}$ | $< 70\text{ A}$ or $> 335\text{ A}$ | $< 50\text{ A}$ or $> 350\text{ A}$ |

### 3.0 PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE BY RUNNING HOURS
Maintenance tasks are cumulative. When a higher hour milestone is reached, all lower-hour tasks shall also be executed. Running hours are tracked via the robot controller internal PLC register `R[902]`.

#### 3.1 Interval: Every 250 Operating Hours (Shift Level)
1. Inspect the welding torch contact tip and gas nozzle for spatter accumulation; clean using pneumatic reamer station and apply anti-spatter fluid (VPW part W-SPAT-01).
2. Check the wire feed drive rolls for particulate debris and groove wear; blow out with dry instrument air at $< 0.3\text{ MPa}$.
3. Verify operation of the TCP (Tool Center Point) calibration routine using the fixed reference jig. Deviation exceeding $\pm 0.5\text{ mm}$ requires reprogramming.
4. Inspect external cabling, teach pendant umbilical, and torch dress package for chafing or insulation cuts.

#### 3.2 Interval: Every 1,000 Operating Hours (Weekly Level)
1. Grease the wire feeder push-pull gear assembly using lithium complex grease (VPW part LUB-WELD-08).
2. Inspect mechanical hard stops on Axes 1 through 6 for impact scoring or deformation.
3. Clean the cabinet air intake filters on the controller and welding power source using vacuum extraction.
4. Check battery backup voltage for absolute encoders; replace if voltage reads below $2.85\text{ VDC}$ (Nominal $3.0\text{ VDC}$).

#### 3.3 Interval: Every 4,000 Operating Hours (Semi-Annual Level)
1. Inspect and purge harmonic drives on Axes 4, 5, and 6; replenish with synthetic harmonic grease (VPW part LUB-ARM-12) per volume matrix in Section 6.0.
2. Verify servo motor brake holding torque on all six axes using the controller static brake test utility. Minimum acceptable holding torque is $125\%$ of rated motor stall torque.
3. Inspect internal hollow-wrist cable harness for twisting or insulation fatigue.
4. Calibrate analog shielding gas mass flow controller against secondary standard meter.

#### 3.4 Interval: Every 12,000 Operating Hours (Annual Overhaul Level)
1. Complete flush and refill of RV reduction gearboxes on Axes 1, 2, and 3 using synthetic gear oil (VPW part LUB-RV-20).
2. Replace timing belts on Axis 2 and Axis 3 drives. Tensioning must be set to $45\text{ Hz}$ vibration frequency using acoustic tension meter.
3. Perform full insulation resistance (Megger) test on all servo motor windings at $500\text{ VDC}$; minimum acceptable resistance is $100\text{ M}\Omega$.
4. Replace main interface relays and safety contactors inside the controller cabinet.

### 4.0 FAULT-CODE TABLE AND DIAGNOSTICS
The following table details named fault conditions generated by the ROBOT-WELD controller, their root causes, and mandatory corrective actions.

**Table 4.1: Fault-Code Diagnostics**

| Fault Code & Name | Probable Cause | Corrective Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ERR-WLD-101: Wire Burnback Detected** | Contact tip fusion due to erratic wire feed speed, incorrect burnback timer setting, or low shielding gas flow causing immediate arc instability. | Inspect contact tip bore diameter; replace if worn by $> 15\%$. Verify gas flow rate at nozzle using rotameter. Clean or replace wire feed drive rolls. Adjust burnback time parameter `P[14]` by $+0.05\text{ s}$ increments. |
| **ERR-WLD-102: Servo Overcurrent Axis 3** | Mechanical binding in Axis 3 harmonic drive, excessive payload inertia, or debris lodged in the lower arm casting cavity. | Perform manual brake release and hand-rotate Axis 3 to check for binding. Inspect gearbox backlash and housing temperature. Clear any foreign obstruction. If binding persists, replace harmonic reducer assembly. |
| **ERR-WLD-103: Gas Pressure Interlock Open** | Primary regulator failure, upstream supply bottle depletion, or pinched flexible feed line inside the upper arm dress pack. | Check primary regulator delivery pressure. Verify supply manifold pressure. Inspect flexible polyurethane gas line for kinks or crushing along the J3-J6 arm routing. Replace damaged hose sections. |
| **ERR-WLD-104: Encoder Communication Loss J5** | Pulse coder cable failure, loose MIL-spec connector at J5 motor base, or electrical noise coupling from welding high-frequency arc starter. | Power down controller. Inspect, clean, and reseat the 20-pin encoder connector at Axis 5 motor. Check shield grounding wire integrity at both ends. Verify continuity of internal wiring harness cores. |
| **ERR-WLD-105: Collision Sensor Tripped** | Mechanical torch collision with fixture, workpiece distortion, or incorrect approach vector causing pneumatic switch separation. | Inspect torch alignment and TCP calibration. Clear physical obstruction. Reset the pneumatic breakaway switch plunger. If optical proximity sensor on collision plate is misaligned, readjust bracket and retighten fasteners. |
| **ERR-WLD-106: Arc Failure to Ignite** | Uncleaned base metal surface, excessive distance between contact tip and workpiece, or worn contact tip producing unstable arc initiation. | Inspect workpiece joint for rust, oil, or thick mill scale; re-clean as per welding specification. Verify TCP height parameter. Replace pitted or oversized contact tip. Check earth ground clamp connection to welding table. |

### 5.0 TORQUE SPECIFICATIONS
All threaded fasteners in structural, motor, and transmission locations on ROBOT-WELD units shall be tightened using calibrated torque wrenches in accordance with Table 5.1. Threads must be clean and dry unless threadlocker application is specified.

**Table 5.1: Fastener Torque Matrix**

| Fastener Location / Description | Thread Size & Grade | Torque Limit ($\text{Nm}$) | Application Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Axis 1 Base Mounting Bolts** | M16, Grade 12.9 | $210\text{ Nm} \pm 5\%$ | Apply Loctite 271; star pattern tightening sequence. |
| **Axis 2 & 3 Reducer Flange Bolts** | M12, Grade 12.9 | $95\text{ Nm} \pm 3\%$ | Apply Loctite 243; cross-torque in 3 stages. |
| **Axis 4, 5, 6 Wrist Housing Bolts** | M6, Grade 10.9 | $12\text{ Nm} \pm 2\%$ | Apply Loctite 243; titanium-coated housing threads. |
| **Servo Motor Mounting Fasteners** | M8, Grade 10.9 | $28\text{ Nm} \pm 3\%$ | Dry assembly. |
| **Welding Torch Clamp Bolt** | M8, Grade 8.8 | $22\text{ Nm} \pm 5\%$ | Ensure insulated collar is seated prior to torque. |
| **TCP Reference Jig Retaining Screws** | M5, Grade 8.8 | $8\text{ Nm} \pm 5\%$ | Dry assembly. |

### 7.0 SAFETY-CRITICAL CORRECTIVE ACTIONS (LOCKOUT & PERMIT REQUIRED)
Execution of the following maintenance and corrective actions alters the safety envelope of the ROBOT-WELD asset. These tasks **strictly require** the issuance of a formal Maintenance Work Permit (Form VPW-MWP-09), complete execution of Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) per Plant Procedure VPW-SAF-001, and verification of zero energy state prior to tool contact.

1. **Replacement or internal wiring repair of Axis 1 through Axis 3 servo motors or electromagnetic brakes.** (Involves gravitational hazard; mechanical drop pin must be inserted into the Axis 2 transport locking bore before work commences).
2. **Disassembly, repair, or replacement of Axis 1, 2, or 3 reduction gearboxes or timing belt drive assemblies.**
3. **Welding power source internal high-voltage capacitor bank discharge and internal inverter module replacement.** (Requires verification of $< 15\text{ VDC}$ residual bus voltage using a CAT III / CAT IV calibrated multimeter).
4. **Replacement of the internal hollow-wrist cable harness (Dress Pack), involving the disconnection of main power, control, gas, and water lines through the J3 to J6 arm assembly.**
5. **Modification, bypassing, or hardwire testing of the safety fence interlock circuit, optical light curtains, or emergency stop chain connected to the robot controller safety PLC.**
